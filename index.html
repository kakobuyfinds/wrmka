Buying through CSSBuy and checking QC photos

From catalog entry to seller link

  1. Open the catalog listing and look for the original seller URL, for example from Weidian, Taobao or 1688.
  2. Paste the seller URL into CSSBuy. Confirm the listing loads and check the selected color, size, quantity and price.
  3. Before paying, check domestic delivery, the agent's charges, and the conditions for requesting a return or exchange.

What to check at the warehouse

  • Shoes: Check both size labels, sole condition and measured insole length if available.
  • Clothing: Compare chest width, length and sleeve measurements with a similar garment you already own.
  • Bags: Inspect the closures, seams and strap attachment points. Request a close-up if an important detail is missing.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Can I paste a HipobuyFind link into CSSBuy?

Use the original seller URL from the catalog listing. Verify that CSSBuy can load it and that the item options match before placing an order.

Why does checkout show a higher amount?

The catalog shows reference item prices. Domestic postage, exchange rates, agent fees and international delivery can change the amount you pay. Your destination may also affect taxes and duties.

How do I compare sizes from different sellers?

Compare the measurements for the exact variant. Match garment measurements with garment measurements, or insole length with insole length, instead of relying on a size label alone.
